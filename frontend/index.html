How to use EmoSense
  1. Choose Start camera and review the participation notice.
  2. Allow camera access. Face the camera in even lighting and keep others out of view unless they agree.
  3. Explore expressions. A result is an estimate of visible features, not your feelings.
  4. Choose Stop camera at any time. Switching tabs also stops the camera.

Cloud snapshots are optional and need a separate confirmation for every image.
